
# Research

This is a home for submissions that do not beat any World Records yet, but are interesting and can beat the record with a bit more effort.

The goal is for the community to work on the solutions here in public to push them over the edge.

- [Loss-budget Gaussian noise](loss_budget/README.md): gated baseline, spherical
  directions, Muon/AdamW, adaptive noise and prediction ensembles.
